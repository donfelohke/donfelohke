## Working principles

- **Human authority:** people retain authorship, decision rights and responsibility.
- **Libre-first:** prefer free software, open standards and interoperability; GNU is foundational, not an exclusive boundary.
- **Local-first:** prioritise privacy, offline capability, user control and durable ownership.
- **Do not recreate wheels:** evaluate existing scholarship, standards, projects and hardware; reuse, adapt and credit creators.
- **Reproducibility:** preserve sources, versions, methods, tests, outcomes and limitations.
- **Honest maturity:** distinguish concept, research, prototype, implementation, validation and deployment.
- **Contextual precision:** respect distinct peoples, histories, languages, knowledge systems and individual experience.
