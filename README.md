## Working principles

- **Human authority:** people retain decision rights and responsibility.
- **Libre-first:** prefer free software, open standards and interoperability; GNU is foundational, not an exclusionary boundary.
- **Local-first:** prioritise privacy, offline capability, user control and durable ownership.
- **Do not recreate wheels:** evaluate existing research and projects, reuse and adapt where appropriate, and credit creators.
- **Reproducibility:** record sources, versions, methods, tests, outcomes and limitations.
- **Honest maturity:** distinguish concept, research, prototype, implementation, validation and deployment.
