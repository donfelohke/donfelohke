# AI Assistance and Portfolio Provenance

**Status:** Proposed profile-repository policy  
**Canonical policy:** [FELOH Corpus — AI-Assisted Development and Source Integrity](https://github.com/donfelohke/feloh-corpus/blob/main/AI-ASSISTED-DEVELOPMENT.md)

This profile repository is a human-authored portfolio index. AI tools may assist drafting or editing, but must not fabricate credentials, projects, collaborations, releases, metrics, testimonials or implementation status.

- Preserve human authorship and review all published claims.
- Link factual project claims to the relevant repository, release, commit or other evidence.
- Disclose material AI assistance when it materially shapes substantive portfolio content.
- Never use an LLM to disguise copied or unauthorised source material.
- Do not place private customer information, credentials or confidential correspondence into external AI tools.
- Keep project maturity accurate: research is not a released product; documentation is not deployment; an enquiry is not a confirmed booking.
- Preserve third-party attribution and asset-specific rights.

This file does not grant a software or content licence to the profile README or linked project assets.


## Portfolio provenance and authorship integrity

Portfolio claims must distinguish personal authorship, collaboration, upstream contribution, AI assistance, research and implementation. Do not present an AI rewrite of another person's code, a copied template or a derivative project as original work. Link substantial project claims to repositories, commits, releases or other evidence; identify upstream projects and collaborators where relevant. Do not use similarity scores, generated explanations or polished prose as proof of originality. If authorship or rights are disputed or uncertain, qualify or withhold the claim until reviewed.
