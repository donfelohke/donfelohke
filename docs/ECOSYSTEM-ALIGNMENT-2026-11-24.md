## Repository roles

The FELOH Corpus is the shared source of record. Specialist repositories own their detailed practice and implementation records. Gallery governs asset-level provenance, rights, consent and approval. dwl-guile and home-service-dwl-guile are supporting WARENA technical works, not additional practice domains.

**ONLY NAKEBUSEN TRANSACTS.** WARENA, IKAI and GENJE are not separate checkout or booking interfaces. “GENJE ZA WARENA | NAKEBUSEN” is a shared creative/public signature, not a merger of the practices.
